## Question

1. How does reported PM vary with relative humidity for low-cost citizen sensors, and can a
   simple correction reduce the humidity-driven bias?
2. How do citizen sensors agree with nearby reference-grade stations, expressed as bias and
   error distributions (p50/p90/p95) rather than a single summary statistic?
